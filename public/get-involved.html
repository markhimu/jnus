Why partner with JNUS?

Localisation works best with partners who are trusted by communities and ready for accountability. That's us.

  • Deep community access

    17 years in Cox's Bazar, with women staff and volunteers in host communities and nine camps.

  • Proven delivery

    25+ programmes for UN Women, FCDO, ECHO, JICA, DRC, BRAC, USAID, Australian Aid and others.

  • Strong compliance

    22 written policies, MEAL systems, safeguarding/PSEA and an internal audit function.

  • Connected to global networks

    Members of CIVICUS, ICVA, Start Network and the Generation Equality WPS-HA Compact.
