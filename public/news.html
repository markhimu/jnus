Dialogues, trainings, campaigns and emergency responses — the everyday work of women changing Cox's Bazar.

Featured · Advocacy2025 · Gulshan, Dhaka

Young women take on tech-facilitated violence

JNUS organised a national dialogue on "Preventing Technology-Facilitated Gender-Based Violence (TFGBV) in Bangladesh: Views from the Young Women Leaders Forum".

Ms. Sharmeen S Murshid, Honourable Adviser to the Ministry of Women and Children Affairs, joined as chief guest as young women from Cox's Bazar shared their experience of online harassment — and their recommendations for safer digital spaces.

WASHJul 2026 · Camp 3, Ukhiya

SHIFT launches: sustainable WASH for 43,170 people

With FCDO and BRAC, JNUS begins the SHIFT project across every block of Camp 3 — strengthening water networks, sanitation and menstrual hygiene for 8,894 households.

Research7 Oct 2025 · Cox's Bazar

Co-analysis workshop: Enhancing resilience in coastal communities

Community members, researchers and practitioners came together at Hotel Uni Resort to make sense of new evidence on climate, conflict and displacement along Bangladesh's coast.

TrainingWith GWI · GWU

Building GBV evidence in humanitarian settings

With the Global Women's Institute at George Washington University, JNUS trained women responders to collect safe, ethical evidence on gender-based violence in crisis settings.
