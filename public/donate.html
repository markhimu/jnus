Transparency

Your trust, accounted for.

Foreign donations to NGOs in Bangladesh are regulated under the Foreign Donations (Voluntary Activities) Regulation Act, 2016 and overseen by the NGO Affairs Bureau. JNUS receives, uses and reports every foreign-funded taka through that system.

  • Annual external audit and published annual reports
  • Internal Audit Team and written financial, procurement and anti-corruption policies
  • Safeguarding, PSEA and complaints response mechanisms
  • Trusted by UN Women, FCDO, ECHO, JICA, BRAC and more

Donor FAQs

Questions, answered

Can't find what you need? Email jagonariunnayon@gmail.com or call +88 01823 929 075.

How do I donate from outside Bangladesh?

Make an international wire (SWIFT) transfer in US dollars to our bank account using the details above — from your online banking, your bank branch, or a licensed money-transfer service. Then send us the short form so we can confirm receipt.

Can I give monthly?

Yes. Ask your bank to set up a recurring international transfer (standing order) to our account, and let us know through the form. Monthly gifts are the most powerful way to support long-term work like women's centres and survivor support.

Will I receive a receipt?

Yes. Once your transfer reaches our account we email you a written acknowledgement with the amount received and the date.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

JNUS is registered in Bangladesh. Tax rules differ by country, and gifts to overseas organisations are often not deductible. Please check with a tax adviser in your country.

How is my money used?

Unrestricted gifts go where the need is greatest across our programmes: survivor support, women's centres, skills training, WASH and emergency response. If you'd like your gift to support a specific area, add a note in the form and we will honour it wherever possible.

Is it safe and legal to send money to a Bangladeshi NGO?

Yes. Foreign donations to NGOs in Bangladesh flow through regulated banking channels and are approved and monitored by the NGO Affairs Bureau. JNUS has managed foreign-funded programmes for UN agencies and international donors for years.

Can I pay by card or PayPal?

At the moment we accept international bank transfers. If a bank transfer isn't possible for you, please contact us and we'll help you find the easiest option.
