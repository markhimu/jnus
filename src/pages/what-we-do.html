Safety, skills, voice — and lasting change.

Eight connected areas of work, one goal: a Cox's Bazar where every woman and girl is safe, independent and in the lead.

01

Protection & GBV response

Through Multi-Purpose Women Centres in Camp 3 (Ukhiya) and Whykong Union (Teknaf), we offer survivors of gender-based violence a confidential place to be heard — and a pathway to safety, justice and healing.

  • Case management & safe referrals
  • Legal aid & gender-responsive ADR
  • Psychosocial support
  • Child protection
  • Training police & local government
  • Community-based protection

With UN Women · Sida · Danish Refugee Council · FCDO · ECHO · FON & CREA

02

Gender equality & women's leadership

We invest in women and girls as leaders — in their families, their camps and villages, and in national policy. Our Young Women Leaders Forum (YWLF) trains a new generation to speak up on GBV, peace and digital safety.

  • Multi-Purpose Women Centres (Camps 3 & 5, Teknaf)
  • Young Women Leaders Forum
  • Women, Peace & Security (WPS) agenda
  • Accelerated Adult Learning (AALP)

With UN Women · Sida · SKUS · GNWP · BNPS

03

Skills & livelihoods

Income is protection. Since 2019 we have trained women in Ramu, Ukhiya, Teknaf and Sadar in marketable trades — and connected them to buyers through Panchali, our social business for women-made products.

Means to Lead

With UN Women in Teknaf and Camps 3 & 5: tailoring, handicraft, dry-fish processing and driving — expanded to baking and beauty in 2025.

PRISE

With BRAC: mobile servicing, IT support, tailoring, beauty, AC/fridge and TV repair, welding and furniture making.

Gender-responsive WASH

As the focal WASH organisation for Camp 3 in Ukhiya, we keep water networks running, latrines safe and lit, and menstrual hygiene dignified — designed with women's safety at the centre.

05

Health for every family

Primary health services for camp and host communities — check-ups, vaccinations and minor treatment — alongside health education on hygiene, disease prevention and family planning.

Emergency response · 2023

After the Kutupalong camp fire, we reached 600 people with Americares — including hygiene kits for 500 women and girls and nutrition supplements for 150 pregnant women.

Climate justice & disaster risk reduction

On Bangladesh's front line of climate change, women lose the most when cyclones and salinity strike. We help them adapt — and lead.

  • Female farmers in Ukhiya learn climate-resilient cultivation techniques.
  • Women fish entrepreneurs use solar fish dryers and egg incubators — 200 women in 10 groups with CFLI.
  • FiLEP with JICA: gender training for tilapia, seaweed, seafood and dry-fish producers.
  • EMPOWER: women-led CSOs advancing climate justice with UN Women and MJF.
07

Peace & social cohesion

Using a human-security approach, we bring host and Rohingya communities together and work to prevent radicalisation and violent extremism — with women at the heart of every dialogue.

  • 13 social cohesion forums (7 in camps)
  • Training for youth, media, teachers & religious leaders
  • Youth digital literacy & anti-cyberbullying
  • Local Women Lead for Sustainable Peace

With NED · USAID/MSI · GNWP · SDC/BRAC

08

Research & advocacy

We turn women's lived experience into evidence — on GBV, female humanitarian workers' safety, and climate-driven conflict and displacement in coastal and border communities — and take it to decision-makers.

Research partners include the Global Women's Institute (GWU), ODI, UN Women, MJF, CFLI and Turning Tide.
